# Indoor plant watering guide

This document is sample material for the minimal RAG. All the data is made up
and is only there to test chunk retrieval.

## Watering the cactus

A cactus needs very little water. Water it every two weeks in summer and once a
month in winter. The soil must dry out completely between waterings: if the
substrate stays wet, the root rots.

## Watering the fern

Ferns are indoor plants that need constant humidity. Water them every three or
four days, and they do well in a bathroom with indirect light. Never leave the
substrate waterlogged.

## Substrates and drainage

Succulents store water in their leaves, so they tolerate drought. The substrate
must drain well: a mix of perlite, coarse sand and peat in a 2:1:1 ratio.

## Temperature and light

The ideal temperature is around 18-24 degrees. Keeping the plants near a window
with filtered light avoids direct sun, which burns the leaves.
